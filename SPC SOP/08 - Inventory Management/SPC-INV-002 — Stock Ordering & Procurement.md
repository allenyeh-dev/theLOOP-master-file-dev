## Purpose

To define how SPC ingredients and supplies are ordered — including who places orders, what supplier contacts are used, and how LOOP Central supplies are requested.

---

## Who Places Orders

The Head Chef is responsible for all ordering decisions at SPC. The Sous Chef may place orders with Head Chef approval. Line staff do not order independently.

---

## Order Triggers

Orders are placed when:
- The daily inventory check (SPC-INV-001) identifies items at or below par level
- Service usage suggests an item will run out before the next scheduled delivery
- A menu change or upcoming event requires additional ingredients

Orders are not placed reactively in the middle of service — anticipation is the standard.

---

## External Suppliers

The Head Chef maintains a supplier contact list. For each key ingredient category, there is a primary supplier and (where possible) a backup supplier in case of supply issues.

Orders are placed by phone, WhatsApp, or supplier portal — the Head Chef uses whatever channel the supplier requires.

When placing an order:
- Confirm item names and quantities clearly
- Confirm the expected delivery date and time
- Confirm pricing if there has been any recent change
- Retain confirmation (message, email, or note in the order log)

---

## LOOP Central (央廚) Requests

Items produced by LOOP Central are requested through the Head Chef to the LOOP Central kitchen manager. The request is made with enough lead time for LOOP Central to schedule production.

When requesting from LOOP Central:
- Specify item name, quantity, and required delivery date
- LOOP Central confirms whether the quantity and timing are feasible
- Any shortfalls are communicated back to the Head Chef in advance so substitute arrangements can be made

---

## Order Log

The Head Chef maintains an order log recording:
- Date ordered
- Supplier
- Items and quantities ordered
- Expected delivery date
- Actual delivery date (noted on receipt)
- Any discrepancies

---

## Emergency Orders

If an item runs out unexpectedly mid-service (e.g., a delivery shortfall or higher-than-expected demand):
1. The supervisor immediately notifies the Head Chef
2. The Head Chef decides whether to 86 the item (SPC-OPS-003) or source an emergency supply
3. Emergency sourcing (e.g., purchasing from a local market) requires Head Chef approval and is documented
