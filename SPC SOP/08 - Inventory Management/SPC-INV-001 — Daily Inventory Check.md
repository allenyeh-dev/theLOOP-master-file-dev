## Purpose

To define the daily inventory check procedure at SPC — ensuring the kitchen always has what it needs for service, expired or deteriorating items are caught early, and ordering decisions are based on accurate stock counts.

---

## When the Daily Check Happens

The daily inventory check is completed at the start of every shift as part of the kitchen opening procedure (SPC-OPS-001). It is done before service begins and before any prep starts.

---

## Cold Storage Check

The opening supervisor checks all refrigerators and cold storage units:

- [ ] All items have a label with item name and prep/received date
- [ ] Any items at or past their use-by date are identified and removed — follow **SPC-FSH-009 — Expired Ingredient Handling**
- [ ] FIFO is confirmed — older dates at the front
- [ ] Nothing is unlabelled — any unlabelled item is either identified and labelled or discarded
- [ ] Temperature reading logged (≤7°C required) — see **SPC-KIT-002**

---

## Freezer Check

- [ ] Freezer is at ≤-18°C — temperature logged
- [ ] All frozen items are solid — no partially thawed items
- [ ] Items are labelled and within their frozen shelf life
- [ ] FIFO applied

---

## Dry Store Check

- [ ] All opened dry goods are in sealed containers and labelled with the opening date
- [ ] No items stored on the floor (minimum 15cm on shelving)
- [ ] No signs of moisture, pest activity, or spoilage
- [ ] Expiry dates on packaged goods are within range

---

## Par Levels & Low Stock

The Head Chef sets par levels for all critical ingredients. Par levels are the minimum stock level that must be on hand before ordering is triggered.

During the daily check, the supervisor compares current stock to par levels and flags any items that are below par or will be below par after today's service.

Low stock items are reported to the Head Chef for ordering — see **SPC-INV-002 — Stock Ordering & Procurement**.

---

## 86 Trigger

If any item is fully depleted and cannot be ordered or substituted in time for service, the item is 86'd before service opens. See **SPC-OPS-003 — 86 Item Procedure**.

---

## End-of-Service Spot Check

At the end of each service, the supervisor does a quick spot check of:
- Items that were heavily used during service — are levels adequate for the next shift?
- Any items that were 86'd — are they flagged for the ordering list?
- Any new items that need labelling after service prep

This feeds directly into the next day's opening check and ordering list.
