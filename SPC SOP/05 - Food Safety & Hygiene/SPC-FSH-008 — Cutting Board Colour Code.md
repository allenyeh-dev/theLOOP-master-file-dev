## Purpose

To define SPC's cutting board colour code — the system that prevents cross-contamination between raw proteins, produce, seafood, and cooked/ready-to-eat foods.

---

## Colour Code Reference

![Colour-coded cutting boards: WHITE / GREEN / BLUE / RED with food type labels](../images/05-fsh/05-fsh_011.png)
*Caption: SPC's cutting board colour code. Four colours are in active use. Every staff member must be able to identify the correct board without referring to documentation. This is one of the first things trained during kitchen orientation.*

| Board Colour | Food Type | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **RED** | Raw meat | Beef, pork, lamb, chicken (raw) |
| **GREEN** | Fresh vegetables & fruit | All fresh produce, herbs |
| **BLUE** | Raw seafood | Fish, prawns, crab, squid |
| **WHITE** | Cooked food & ready-to-eat items | Cooked proteins, assembled dishes, sliced bread |

---

## Rules

**One board per food type — always.**
The colour of the board defines what is cut on it. This is not flexible and does not change based on urgency or convenience.

**No exceptions.**
If the correct colour board is not clean and available:
- Clean and sanitise an available board before use (75% alcohol, clean cloth, allow to dry), or
- Notify the supervisor — they will organise a clean board
- Do not use the wrong colour board in the meantime

**Washing between uses.**
A cutting board that has been used for raw protein is never used for another task after just a quick rinse. It must go through the dishwasher before reuse.

**End-of-shift dishwasher.**
All cutting boards go through the dishwasher at the end of every shift, regardless of whether they were used. Boards are stored dry, upright where possible, with space between them to allow airflow.

---

## Board Condition

Cutting boards are inspected during the weekly deep clean:
- Deep knife scoring, cracks, or permanent staining → board is retired and replaced
- Warped boards that do not lie flat → retired and replaced
- Any board with rough texture that cannot be cleaned thoroughly → retired and replaced

A replacement board is sourced by the supervisor within one business day. While waiting for a replacement, the Head Chef assigns tasks to avoid that colour shortfall.

---

## Training Requirement

All new staff are trained on the cutting board colour code during kitchen orientation (Day 1 of onboarding — see SPC-TRN-001). Competency is assessed by the supervisor as part of the training sign-off. Any staff member observed using the wrong colour board is corrected immediately and retrained.
