## Purpose

To define the food safety requirements for preparation and cooking at SPC — covering produce washing, protein handling, and the use of personal protective equipment during prep.

---

## Produce Washing

All fresh produce is washed before preparation regardless of whether the outer skin or leaves will be removed.

![Gloved hands washing produce in a bowl of clean water](../images/05-fsh/05-fsh_007.jpg)
*Caption: Fresh produce is washed in a clean bowl or sink of running water before prep. Gloves are worn throughout. Produce is not rinsed under a trickle — it is immersed and agitated to remove soil, bacteria, and surface residues.*

**Produce washing procedure:**

1. Hands washed (SPC-FSH-001) and clean gloves put on before handling produce
2. Bowl or sink rinsed clean before filling with water
3. Produce immersed in clean water and agitated — leafy vegetables are separated into individual leaves where possible
4. Produce drained and placed on a clean surface or in a clean GN pan — never placed directly on the floor
5. Washing water discarded after each batch — not reused

---

## Protein Handling & Labelling

Raw proteins (meat, poultry, seafood) are handled separately from all other food items and are stored and labelled correctly before being placed in cold storage.

![Packaged proteins wrapped and labelled on trays in cold storage](../images/05-fsh/05-fsh_008.jpg)
*Caption: Raw proteins are wrapped, labelled with item name and date, and stored on trays to prevent drip contamination. No raw protein is placed on open shelves or in contact with other items without a sealed container or wrap.*

**Protein handling requirements:**

- Raw proteins are handled on the designated raw-protein cutting board only (RED — see SPC-FSH-008)
- All packaging is removed at the designated raw-protein prep area — never at other workbenches
- Proteins are portioned, wrapped, and labelled with item name and date before cold storage
- Any packaging or wrapping from raw proteins is discarded immediately — not left on workbenches
- Hands and all contact surfaces are washed after handling raw proteins before moving to any other task

---

## Gloves During Food Preparation

Disposable gloves are worn during:
- Washing produce
- Handling raw proteins
- Handling ready-to-eat food that will not be cooked further
- Any task where hands have a wound or break in skin (see SPC-FSH-001)

Gloves are changed whenever:
- Switching between raw and cooked/ready-to-eat tasks
- Gloves become torn, soiled, or contaminated
- Leaving the station for any reason and returning

Wearing gloves does not replace handwashing. Hands are washed before putting on new gloves.

---

## Allergen Awareness

Where SPC menu items contain common allergens (shellfish, gluten, dairy, eggs, nuts), staff must:
- Not substitute or swap ingredients without Head Chef approval
- Alert the Head Chef if a guest requests allergen information or an allergen modification
- Never claim a dish is allergen-free without explicit Head Chef confirmation

The Head Chef maintains the current allergen matrix for the SPC menu and ensures it is communicated to all staff.

---

## Sauce & Condiment Preparation

- Sauces made in batch are cooled rapidly before cold storage — spread into shallow GN pans to accelerate cooling, covered once below 40°C
- All sauce containers are labelled with item name and prep date (see SPC-FSH-006)
- Sauces that have been held at service temperature for more than the Head Chef's specified hold time are discarded at the end of service
