## Purpose

To define the labelling and storage standards for all ingredients and prepped items at SPC — ensuring all food is traceable, stored correctly, and used within safe timeframes.

---

## Labelling Requirements

Every prepped item and every open or decanted ingredient must be labelled before being placed in cold storage, a shelf, or any storage area.

![Labelled sauce containers stored on a stainless shelf — each container tagged with contents and date](../images/05-fsh/05-fsh_012.png)
*Caption: Sauce containers after prep — every container is labelled with the item name and prep date before being stored. Labels are placed on the container, not the lid. The label is legible at a glance so any staff member can identify the item and check its date without opening the container.*

**Every label must include:**
- Item name (in English or Chinese — whichever is consistently understood by all staff)
- Prep date
- Use-by date (calculated from the Head Chef's shelf-life reference — see below)
- Staff member's initials (recommended)

Labels are placed on the container body, not the lid. Lids are exchanged between containers — labels on lids will be misread.

---

## Shelf Life Reference

The following shelf-life guidelines apply to SPC prepped items held in the refrigerator (≤7°C):

| Item Type | Maximum Cold Storage (from prep date) |
|---|---|
| Cooked sauces and bases | 3 days |
| Cooked proteins | 2 days |
| Prepped raw vegetables (cut) | 2 days |
| Marinated raw proteins | 1–2 days (Head Chef to specify per item) |
| Cooked rice or grains | 1 day |
| Fresh produce (uncut) | As per delivery shelf life |

Where the Head Chef specifies different guidelines for specific SPC menu items, those guidelines override the table above and are posted at the relevant station.

---

## FIFO — First In, First Out

All storage areas operate on FIFO:
- Newer stock goes to the back when items are received or replenished
- Older stock is pulled from the front and used first
- During the opening check, all items are visually rotated — older dates to the front

No item is placed in storage without a label. If an unlabelled item is found:
1. Do not use it
2. Report it to the supervisor
3. The item is either identified with certainty and labelled, or it is discarded

---

## Dry Storage

Dry goods (flours, sauces, spices, packaging materials) are stored:
- Minimum 15cm off the floor on shelving
- In sealed containers after opening — no open bags
- Away from heat sources (ovens, fryers)
- Away from chemicals and cleaning materials (separate storage area)

Labels on dry goods must include the opening date once the original package is opened.

---

## Allergen Separation

Ingredients that are known allergens (nuts, shellfish, gluten-containing items) are stored separately from non-allergen items where feasible, and clearly labelled. The Head Chef decides on allergen storage organisation and communicates it to all staff.

---

## Receiving vs. Storage

Items are labelled at receiving if they will be decanted or transferred into SPC containers. Items in original sealed packaging do not require an additional SPC label, but must be placed in storage in FIFO order.

See **SPC-DEL-001 — Goods Receiving Procedure** for full receiving and inspection standards.
