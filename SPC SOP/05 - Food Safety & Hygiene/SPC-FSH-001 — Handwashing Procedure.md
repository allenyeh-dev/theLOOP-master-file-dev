## Purpose

Correct handwashing is the single most important food safety habit in the kitchen. This SOP defines when and how all SPC staff must wash their hands.

---

## When to Wash Hands

Hands must be washed:

- On arrival at the kitchen before touching anything
- Before handling any food or food contact surfaces
- After using the toilet — no exceptions
- After touching raw meat, poultry, or seafood
- After handling waste or cleaning materials
- After touching the face, hair, or body
- After handling money or personal belongings
- After sneezing or coughing
- Whenever hands may have been contaminated

When in doubt — wash.

## Correct 6-Step Handwashing Procedure

![6-step handwashing infographic: 濕 Wet → 洗 Soap → 刷 Scrub → 搓 Lather → 沖 Rinse → 乾 Dry](../images/05-fsh/05-fsh_001.jpg)
*Caption: The standard 6-step handwashing procedure posted at all SPC handwashing stations — 濕 (wet), 洗 (soap), 刷 (scrub nail brush), 搓 (lather, minimum 20 seconds), 沖 (rinse thoroughly), 乾 (dry with hand dryer or paper towel).*

**Step 1 — 濕 (Wet):** Wet both hands completely under clean running water.

**Step 2 — 洗 (Soap):** Apply cleaning agent evenly to both hands.

**Step 3 — 刷 (Scrub):** Use a clean nail brush to scrub fingertips and under nails.

**Step 4 — 搓 (Lather):** Rub both hands together — palm to palm, back of hands, between fingers, around thumbs — for at least 20 seconds.

**Step 5 — 沖 (Rinse):** Rinse both hands thoroughly under clean running water until all soap is removed.

**Step 6 — 乾 (Dry):** Dry completely with a hand dryer or clean paper towel. Do not wipe hands on clothing or apron.

![Soapy lathered hands — correct technique during Step 4](../images/05-fsh/05-fsh_010.png)
*Caption: Hands fully lathered during the scrubbing step. Minimum 20 seconds of active lathering before rinsing.*

---

## Hand Inspection

Before food handling begins, the supervisor checks all staff hands:

- No watches, rings, bracelets, or jewellery
- Nails short — must not be visible when looking at the palm face-on
- No wounds or cuts exposed — any wound must be covered with a waterproof plaster and a clean disposable glove

---

## Disposable Gloves

Disposable gloves are used when handling ready-to-eat food or when hands have a wound or cut. Gloves do not replace handwashing — hands are washed before gloves are put on.

![Putting on clean disposable gloves before handling ready-to-eat food](../images/05-fsh/05-fsh_009.png)
*Caption: Disposable gloves are put on after handwashing — not instead of it. Gloves are changed whenever they become contaminated or when switching between different food tasks.*
