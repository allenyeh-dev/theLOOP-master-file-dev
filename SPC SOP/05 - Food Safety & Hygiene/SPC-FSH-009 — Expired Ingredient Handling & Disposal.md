## Purpose

To define how expired or deteriorating ingredients are identified, quarantined, and disposed of at SPC — ensuring no expired food enters service under any circumstances.

---

## When an Ingredient is Considered Expired

An ingredient is expired and must be removed from service if any of the following apply:

- The use-by date or SPC prep label date shows the item has exceeded its permitted shelf life
- The item shows visible signs of spoilage: mould, discolouration, slime, or unusual texture
- The item has an off odour that was not present when it was fresh
- The item's packaging is damaged, swollen, or compromised in a way that may have allowed contamination
- The item has been stored outside the required temperature range for more than 2 hours

**When in doubt — discard.** No ingredient is put into service on the basis of "it probably still okay." The decision to use or discard is made by the Head Chef or Sous Chef only.

---

## Discovery During a Shift

If a staff member discovers a potentially expired or deteriorating item during a shift:

1. **Stop** — do not use the item
2. **Isolate** — remove it from the active station immediately; place it in a clearly identified area or container, separate from all food in service
3. **Notify** — tell the Head Chef or Sous Chef immediately
4. **Do not discard independently** — the Head Chef or Sous Chef makes the final disposal decision to ensure the item is logged

---

## Extension Policy

The Head Chef or Sous Chef may extend the use-by date of a fresh ingredient by a maximum of 1 day where:
- The item was received in excellent condition
- It has been stored correctly throughout
- Its physical condition and smell confirm it remains safe and of acceptable quality
- The extension is approved by the Head Chef or Sous Chef in person — not delegated to line staff

Extensions are logged on the item's label with the authorising staff member's initials and the new date.

See **SPC-OPS-007 — Fresh Ingredient Extension Policy** for the full extension procedure.

---

## Disposal Procedure

When an ingredient is confirmed as expired or unsuitable for use:

1. Place the item in a sealed bag or covered container
2. Mark the bag clearly: "DISCARD — [date] — [reason]"
3. Remove from all food storage and food prep areas immediately
4. Dispose of in the kitchen waste bin (not the recycling stream)
5. Log the item in the daily waste log: item name, quantity, reason for disposal, and the staff member who authorised disposal

The Head Chef reviews the waste log weekly to identify patterns (recurring spoilage of the same item may indicate an ordering or storage issue).

---

## Opening Check — Expired Item Sweep

As part of the kitchen opening procedure (SPC-OPS-001), the opening supervisor sweeps all cold storage and dry storage for expired items before service begins. Any items at or past their use-by date are removed, logged, and either extended per SPC-OPS-007 (if applicable) or discarded.

No expired item remains in storage once identified.

---

## Returned Food from Delivery

Food returned by delivery platform customers is not re-plated or re-served under any circumstances. Returned food is discarded immediately. See **SPC-OPS-005 — Guest Complaint & Food Return Handling**.
