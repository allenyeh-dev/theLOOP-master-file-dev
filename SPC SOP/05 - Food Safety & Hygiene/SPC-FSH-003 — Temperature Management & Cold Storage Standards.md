## Purpose

Temperature control is the primary tool for preventing bacterial growth in food. This SOP defines the required temperatures for all cold storage at SPC and the procedures for monitoring and maintaining them.

---

## Temperature Reference Standards

| Storage Type | Required Temperature |
|---|---|
| Refrigerator / chiller | ≤7°C |
| Freezer | ≤-18°C |
| Hot-held food (service) | ≥65°C |
| Cooked food centre | ≥70°C |
| Danger zone — do not hold food here | 7°C – 65°C |

Food left in the danger zone (7°C–65°C) for more than 2 hours is not served and is discarded.

## Reading Cold Storage Temperatures

All SPC cold storage units are fitted with a temperature gauge that must be checked at the start and end of every shift and at the scheduled mid-service check.

## Cold Storage Organisation

Correct food placement inside the refrigerator prevents cross-contamination and ensures even cooling.

![Commercial fridge — GN pans with cooked items on top shelves, raw items on lower shelves](../images/05-fsh/05-fsh_006.jpg)
*Caption: The correct refrigerator organisation at SPC: cooked and ready-to-eat items on the top shelves, raw proteins (meat, poultry, seafood) on the lower shelves. This prevents raw juices from dripping onto cooked food.*

**Shelf organisation — top to bottom:**

| Position | Contents |
|---|---|
| Top shelf | Ready-to-eat food, cooked items, sauces, prepped items |
| Middle shelf | Dairy, eggs, prepared ingredients |
| Lower shelf | Raw meat, raw poultry |
| Bottom shelf / tray | Raw seafood |

Raw proteins are never stored above any cooked or ready-to-eat item.

---

## Temperature Log

A temperature log is maintained for all cold storage units. Readings are recorded at:

| Check | Timing |
|---|---|
| Opening check | Start of every shift |
| Mid-service check | As scheduled by Head Chef (typically every 2–4 hours) |
| Closing check | End of every shift |

If any reading is outside the required range:

1. Do not use the unit until the cause is identified
2. Notify the supervisor immediately
3. Assess whether food stored in the unit is safe — apply the 2-hour/temperature rule
4. If the unit fault cannot be corrected, escalate to the Head Chef
5. Document the issue and any food disposed of in the temperature log

---

## Freezer Management

- Frozen items are stored at ≤-18°C
- No refreezing of previously thawed items
- Defrosting of frozen ingredients is done in the refrigerator (not on the bench at room temperature) unless done under running cold water as a controlled supervised process
- All frozen items are labelled with defrost date when moved from freezer to chiller

---

## Hot-Holding

Food prepared in advance for service that is held hot must be maintained at ≥65°C. If hot-held food drops below 65°C:

- Food may be re-heated once to at least 70°C (probe-verified) and returned to hot-holding
- Food that has been at below 65°C for more than 2 hours is discarded
