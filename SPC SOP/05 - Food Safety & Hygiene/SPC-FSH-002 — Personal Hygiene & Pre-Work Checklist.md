## Purpose

To define the personal hygiene standards all SPC staff must meet before entering the kitchen for any shift. Personal hygiene is the first line of defence against food contamination.

---

## Uniform Standards

All staff must arrive in the correct SPC kitchen uniform and change before entering the food preparation area if coming from outside.

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Chef coat | White, clean and pressed — no tears, stains, or missing buttons |
| Trousers | Black cotton — clean and full length |
| Cap | Black — covering all hair completely |
| Apron | Black — clean, tied correctly |
| Footwear | Non-slip closed-toe shoes — no sandals, open-toe, or cloth shoes |
| Socks | Required — no bare feet in non-slip shoes |

Uniforms are not worn to and from work — they are worn exclusively inside the kitchen.

---

## Jewellery & Accessories

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Rings | Not permitted — wedding bands are the only exception and must be covered with a glove during food handling |
| Watches | Not permitted during service |
| Bracelets, bangles | Not permitted |
| Earrings | Small studs only — large hoops or dangling earrings not permitted |
| Necklaces | Must be tucked inside uniform — not hanging loose |

---

## Hair & Facial Hygiene

- Hair must be fully covered by the cap. No hair should be visible at the front or sides
- Long hair must be tied back before putting on the cap
- Beards must be neat and trimmed. A beard net is required for all facial hair that cannot be kept clear of food
- No strong perfume or fragrance — this can transfer to food

---

## Nails & Skin

- Fingernails must be short — trimmed so they are not visible when looking at the palm face-on
- No nail polish or artificial nails
- Any cut, wound, or skin infection on the hands must be reported to the supervisor before starting work. A blue waterproof plaster and a clean glove are required before food handling is permitted
- Staff with skin infections on the hands may be assigned non-food-contact duties at the supervisor's discretion

---

## Health & Illness

Staff must not work in the kitchen if they have any of the following:
- Diarrhoea or vomiting — regardless of whether it is active or recent (within 48 hours)
- Fever or flu symptoms
- Eye, ear, or skin infections that could contaminate food
- Any open or infected wounds on the hands that cannot be adequately covered

Staff who become ill during a shift must inform the supervisor immediately and step away from food handling.

---

## Pre-Shift Checklist

Before starting any food handling task, every staff member confirms:

- [ ] Uniform clean and complete — coat, trousers, cap, apron, non-slip shoes
- [ ] All jewellery removed or secured
- [ ] Hair fully covered by cap
- [ ] Nails short — no polish or artificial nails
- [ ] No wounds on hands — or wound covered with blue waterproof plaster and clean glove
- [ ] Feeling well — no illness symptoms
- [ ] Hands washed (SPC-FSH-001) immediately before touching any food or food surface

---

## Supervisor Responsibility

The opening supervisor conducts a visual check of all staff personal hygiene at the start of each shift. Any staff member not meeting the above standards is corrected before beginning work. Repeat non-compliance is escalated to the Head Chef.
