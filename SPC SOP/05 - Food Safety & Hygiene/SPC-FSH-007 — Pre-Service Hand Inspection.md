---

## Purpose

To define the supervisor's pre-service hand and personal hygiene inspection procedure — carried out at the start of each shift before any food handling begins.

---

## Why This Inspection Exists

Hands are the primary vector for food contamination. This inspection ensures all staff have completed their pre-shift personal hygiene requirements before touching food, food surfaces, or equipment. It is not a formality — it is the last checkpoint before food safety passes to individual staff responsibility during service.

---

## Inspection Checklist

The opening supervisor inspects each staff member's hands before service:

**Nails:**
- [ ] Nails are short — not visible when looking at the palm face-on
- [ ] No nail polish — natural nails only
- [ ] No artificial nails or nail extensions

**Jewellery:**
- [ ] No rings (wedding bands may be permitted with a glove during food handling — supervisor discretion)
- [ ] No watches, bracelets, or bangles
- [ ] No other jewellery on wrists or hands

**Skin condition:**
- [ ] No visible open cuts, wounds, sores, or skin infections on the hands or wrists
- [ ] Any cut or wound is covered with a blue waterproof plaster, and a clean disposable glove is in place over it

**Handwashing:**
- [ ] Staff confirms they have washed their hands per SPC-FSH-001 immediately before beginning work
- [ ] Hands appear clean and dry — no visible soil, grease, or food residue

---

## Wound Protocol

If a staff member has a cut or wound on their hand:

1. The wound is covered with a **blue** waterproof plaster (blue = visible if it falls into food)
2. A clean disposable glove is worn over the plaster at all times during food handling
3. The glove is checked regularly throughout the shift — replaced immediately if torn or removed
4. If the wound is deep, infected, or cannot be securely covered, the staff member is assigned non-food-contact duties for that shift

---

## Handling Inspection Failures

If a staff member does not meet the inspection requirements:
- Nail polish, artificial nails: the staff member must remove them before starting work (nail polish remover is available in the staff area)
- Jewellery: removed before starting work — items secured in the staff locker
- Exposed wound: blue plaster + glove applied before starting work
- Nails too long: trimmed before starting work

If correction cannot be completed quickly, the supervisor reassigns the staff member to non-food-contact duties until ready.

---

## Frequency

Hand inspection is performed at the start of every shift. For split-shift staff joining mid-service, the supervisor conducts the inspection when the staff member arrives.
