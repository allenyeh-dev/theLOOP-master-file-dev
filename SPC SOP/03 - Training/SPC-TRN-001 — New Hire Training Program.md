| **Version** | 1.0 |

---

## Purpose

To define the structured training program every new SPC team member completes before working independently — covering food safety, equipment, operations, and kitchen standards.

---

## Training Overview

| Phase | Duration | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Orientation | Day 1 | Venue, team, systems, safety |
| Supervised training | Days 1–5 | Food safety, equipment, order flow, stations |
| Independent assessment | Day 5–7 | Supervisor sign-off on all areas |
| Ongoing | Monthly | Food safety refresher, new menu items, process updates |

---

## Phase 1 — Orientation (Day 1)

- [ ] Kitchen layout walkthrough — all stations, storage, equipment locations, emergency exits
- [ ] Meet the team — supervisor introductions, shift structure explained
- [ ] Uniform standards reviewed and uniform issued (SPC-HR-003)
- [ ] Emergency procedures: fire exits, evacuation route, fire extinguisher location, first aid kit
- [ ] The Loop portal access set up and role explained

---

## Phase 2 — Supervised Training (Days 1–5)

**Food Safety & Hygiene (mandatory — must complete before any food handling):**
- [ ] Handwashing procedure (SPC-FSH-001) — demonstrated and practised
- [ ] Personal hygiene and uniform standards (SPC-FSH-002)
- [ ] Temperature management — cold storage, cooking temps, danger zone (SPC-FSH-003)
- [ ] Cross-contamination prevention (SPC-FSH-004)
- [ ] Cutting board colour code (SPC-FSH-005)
- [ ] Ingredient receiving and storage (SPC-FSH-006)
- [ ] Expired ingredient handling (SPC-FSH-009)

**Equipment (supervised until competent):**
- [ ] Equipment opening sequence observed (SPC-EQP-001)
- [ ] Equipment closing sequence observed (SPC-EQP-002)
- [ ] Equipment opening practised independently under supervision
- [ ] Equipment closing practised independently under supervision

**Operations:**
- [ ] Order flow and in-service rules (SPC-OPS-004)
- [ ] 86 item procedure (SPC-OPS-003)
- [ ] Shift handover note reviewed and explained (SPC-OPS-006)
- [ ] Guest complaint procedure (SPC-OPS-005)

**Station Training:**
- [ ] Assigned station(s) trained by Head Chef or senior cook
- [ ] Mise en place standards for each assigned station
- [ ] Product knowledge for items produced at each station

---

## Phase 3 — Independent Assessment (Days 5–7)

Before a new hire works unsupervised, the Head Chef or a designated supervisor confirms the following:

- [ ] Food safety knowledge quiz completed and passed (minimum score: 80%)
- [ ] Equipment opening completed correctly without prompting
- [ ] Equipment closing completed correctly without prompting
- [ ] Station prep completed to standard
- [ ] Order flow rules understood and applied
- [ ] All items signed off — supervisor signature with date

---

## Ongoing Training

- Monthly food safety refresher — reviewed by supervisor at shift briefing
- New menu items: Head Chef demos and signs off before item is served
- Process or procedure updates: briefed at shift start and noted in handover
