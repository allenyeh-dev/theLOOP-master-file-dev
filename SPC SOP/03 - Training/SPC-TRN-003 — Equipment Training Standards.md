## Purpose

To define the equipment training requirements for SPC kitchen staff — ensuring every team member can safely operate, start up, and shut down all kitchen equipment.

---

## Equipment Training Requirements

All kitchen staff must be trained on the equipment they will use. Supervisors must be trained on all equipment.

## Training Method

**Step 1 — Observation:** New staff observe the Head Chef or a trained supervisor performing the full opening or closing sequence.

**Step 2 — Supervised practice:** New staff perform the sequence with the supervisor present and providing guidance.

**Step 3 — Independent assessment:** New staff perform the full sequence without guidance. The supervisor confirms each step is correct before sign-off.

---

## Sign-Off Requirements

No staff member operates any equipment independently until the supervisor has signed off their competency. Sign-off records are filed in the staff member's training record.

---

## Equipment Faults

Staff who encounter an equipment fault during operation must:
- Stop using the equipment immediately
- Notify the supervisor
- Not attempt to repair or adjust the equipment themselves

Supervisor escalates to Head Chef and, if required, building maintenance or a certified technician. See **SPC-EQP-003 — Equipment Fault Reporting**.
