## Purpose

To define the food safety training that all SPC staff must complete and maintain — ensuring the team has the knowledge and habits to prevent foodborne illness and meet Taiwan food safety standards.

---

## Mandatory Training — All Staff

Every team member must complete the following before handling food:

| Topic | SOP Reference | Completion Required |
|---|---|---|
| Correct handwashing procedure | SPC-FSH-001 | Before first shift |
| Personal hygiene & uniform | SPC-HR-003 | Before first shift |
| Temperature management | SPC-FSH-003 | Before first shift |
| Cross-contamination prevention | SPC-FSH-004 | Before first shift |
| Cutting board colour code | SPC-FSH-005 | Before first shift |
| Ingredient receiving & FIFO | SPC-FSH-006 | Before first shift |
| Expired ingredient handling | SPC-FSH-009 | Before first shift |

---

## Mandatory Training — Supervisors

In addition to the above, all supervisors must also complete:

| Topic | SOP Reference |
|---|---|
| Temperature log management | SPC-FSH-003 |
| Fresh ingredient extension policy | SPC-OPS-007 |
| LOOP Central replenishment request | SPC-INV-003 |
| Disposal form procedure | SPC-INV-004 |

---

## Food Safety Knowledge Quiz

All new staff complete a written food safety quiz after completing Phase 2 training:
- Minimum passing score: 80%
- Staff who do not pass are given additional coaching and retested before independent work
- Quiz results are filed in the staff member's training record

---

## Refresher Training

- Monthly food safety refresher briefing — conducted by the supervisor at shift start
- Topics rotate monthly and are chosen by the Head Chef based on recent incidents, common errors, or seasonal considerations
- Refresher topics are noted in the shift handover record

---

## Food Safety Certification

Staff who handle food in a commercial kitchen are encouraged to hold a valid food handling certificate per Taiwan Food Safety and Sanitation Act requirements. The Head Chef tracks certification status for all team members and alerts Ownership before any certificate expires.
