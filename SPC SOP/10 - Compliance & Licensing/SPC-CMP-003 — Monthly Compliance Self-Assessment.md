## Purpose

To define the monthly internal compliance self-assessment — a structured review of SPC's compliance posture across all regulatory and operational areas.

---

## Frequency & Ownership

The monthly compliance self-assessment is conducted once per month by the Head Chef. The completed assessment is reviewed by the F&B Director. Any findings are actioned before the next assessment.

---

## Self-Assessment Checklist

### Licences & Permits
- [ ] All required licences are current and not approaching expiry
- [ ] All licences that require display are posted in the correct location
- [ ] Staff food handler certificates — all current, no lapses

### Temperature Records
- [ ] Temperature logs for the past month are complete — no gaps
- [ ] No unresolved temperature events (out-of-range readings with no documented action)
- [ ] Delivery temperature logs are complete

### Training Records
- [ ] All current staff have completed required food safety training (SPC-TRN-002)
- [ ] Any new staff hired this month have their onboarding training completed or in progress
- [ ] No expired certifications in the team

### Food Safety Operations
- [ ] Cutting board colour code is being observed — spot check confirms correct boards at each station
- [ ] Labelling is current — no unlabelled items found in cold storage or dry store
- [ ] FIFO is being applied — oldest dates at the front
- [ ] Temperature monitoring is happening at the correct intervals
- [ ] Expired ingredient disposal is being logged (waste log up to date)

### Pest Control
- [ ] Pest control visit completed this month (or on scheduled frequency)
- [ ] No unreported pest sightings this month
- [ ] Pest control records filed and available

### Equipment
- [ ] All equipment fault log entries from this month have been resolved or escalated
- [ ] No equipment currently out of service without a plan for repair
- [ ] YAZAKI gas alarm functional — green status confirmed at last opening check

### Cleaning
- [ ] Daily and end-of-shift cleaning is being completed per SPC-KIT-003
- [ ] Weekly deep clean completed this month
- [ ] No unaddressed hygiene findings

### Incident Log
- [ ] Any customer complaints received this month — documented and resolved (SPC-OPS-005)
- [ ] Any food safety incidents this month — documented, investigated, and actioned
- [ ] Any near-misses or concerns raised by staff — documented

---

## Assessment Scoring

For each section above, note:
- **✓ Compliant** — all items in section confirmed clear
- **⚠ Requires Action** — one or more items need attention — describe the gap and set a resolution deadline
- **✗ Non-Compliant** — a regulatory requirement is currently not being met — immediate escalation to F&B Director

---

## Completed Assessment Filing

Completed monthly assessments are filed by the Head Chef and retained for a minimum of 12 months. They are available for review by the F&B Director or in the event of an inspection.
