## Purpose

To define SPC's obligations under Taiwan food safety regulations and the internal standards that ensure the kitchen operates in full compliance at all times.

---

## Regulatory Framework

SPC operates under the jurisdiction of the Taiwan Food Safety Management Act (食品安全衛生管理法) and is subject to inspection by the relevant local health authority (衛生局). All SPC kitchen operations must meet or exceed the standards required under this legislation.

The F&B Director is responsible for maintaining awareness of regulatory changes and ensuring SPC's SOPs are updated accordingly.

---

## Key Regulatory Obligations

### Food Handler Health

All staff handling food must be free of communicable illness. Staff with conditions that present a food safety risk (diarrhoea, vomiting, skin infections on hands) must be stood down from food-handling duties. See **SPC-FSH-002**.

### Food Handler Training

Staff who handle food must have valid food handler training credentials as required by applicable regulations. The Head Chef maintains records of all staff training certifications and ensures renewals are completed on schedule. See **SPC-TRN-002**.

### Temperature Control

The kitchen maintains temperature logs for cold storage and deliveries. Logs are available for inspection at any time. See **SPC-FSH-003** and **SPC-DEL-003**.

### Food Labelling

All food items in storage are labelled with item name and date. This applies to both customer-facing packaging and internal kitchen labelling. See **SPC-FSH-006**.

### Pest Control

The kitchen operates under a pest control programme. Pest control records are maintained and available for inspection. Any pest sighting is reported immediately and addressed within 24 hours.

### Cleaning & Sanitation Records

The kitchen maintains cleaning logs and records. These are available for inspection on request.

---

## Inspection Readiness

SPC maintains inspection readiness at all times — the kitchen is kept to inspection standard every day, not only when an inspection is anticipated.

If an inspector arrives:
1. The Head Chef (or senior staff member present) greets the inspector and remains available throughout
2. All requested records are produced immediately — temperature logs, training records, pest control records, cleaning records
3. The inspector is given full access to all areas of the kitchen
4. Any corrective action required by the inspector is documented and actioned within the timeframe specified

After any inspection, whether routine or surprise:
- The Head Chef reviews all findings with the team
- Any required corrections are actioned immediately
- If a re-inspection is required, the Head Chef coordinates with the F&B Director

---

## Notification Obligations

Certain events must be reported to the relevant authority:
- Food poisoning incidents involving customers (real or suspected) are reported to the local health authority if required by law
- Any food recall affecting SPC ingredients is actioned immediately — affected stock is removed and the authority is notified as required

The F&B Director is the decision-maker on all regulatory notification obligations.
