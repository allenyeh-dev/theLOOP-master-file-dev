## Purpose

To define the licensing and permit requirements for SPC to legally operate as a food business in Taiwan, and to define who is responsible for maintaining each licence.

---

## Required Licences & Permits

| Licence / Permit | Issuing Authority | Renewal Frequency | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Food Business Registration (食品業者登錄) | FDA / Local Health Authority | Annual | F&B Director |
| Commercial Kitchen Establishment Certificate | Local Health Authority | As required | F&B Director |
| Building Use Permit (for kitchen use) | Local Government | As required | F&B Director |
| Gas Appliance Safety Inspection | Certified Inspector / Utility | Per local requirement | Head Chef + F&B Director |
| Fire Safety Certificate / Equipment Inspection | Fire Authority | Annual | F&B Director |
| Food Handler Certificates (individual staff) | Accredited Training Body | Per staff schedule | Head Chef |

This table is a reference guide. The F&B Director maintains the definitive licensing schedule and is responsible for ensuring no licence lapses.

---

## Licence Display

Any licence or certificate required by law to be displayed on the premises is posted in the visible location specified by the issuing authority. The Head Chef confirms licences are posted and current during the monthly compliance check (SPC-CMP-003).

---

## Renewal Process

The F&B Director maintains a licence renewal calendar. At least 30 days before any licence expiry:
1. The F&B Director initiates the renewal process
2. Any required inspections are scheduled
3. Renewal fees are paid before the expiry date
4. The renewed certificate is filed and any display copy is updated

No licence is allowed to lapse. Operating without a valid required licence is a regulatory violation.

---

## New Licence Applications

If SPC adds a new activity, equipment, or location that requires a new licence or approval (e.g., adding a new gas appliance, expanding kitchen footprint, adding a new delivery brand under a new legal entity), the F&B Director assesses the licensing implications before the activity begins.

---

## Staff Food Handler Certificates

All staff who handle food are required to hold valid food handler certification as required by Taiwan regulation. The Head Chef:
- Maintains a record of all staff certificates and their expiry dates
- Schedules renewal training before any certificate expires
- Does not allow an expired-certificate staff member to handle food until the renewal is complete
