## Purpose

SPC receives prepared and semi-prepared items from LOOP Central (央廚), the theLOOP Productions group production kitchen. This SOP defines how these deliveries are received, verified, and integrated into daily kitchen operations.

---

## About LOOP Central

LOOP Central is the central production kitchen for theLOOP Productions group. It supplies SPC with batch-produced items — cooked bases, sauces, marinated proteins, pre-portioned items, and other kitchen prep that is produced centrally and distributed to SPC for finishing and service.

LOOP Central is not a third-party supplier — it is an internal operation. However, the same food safety standards apply to LOOP Central deliveries as to any external supplier. No item from LOOP Central bypasses the receiving inspection.

---

## Delivery Schedule

LOOP Central deliveries are scheduled by the Head Chef in coordination with the LOOP Central kitchen manager. The expected delivery schedule is communicated to the opening supervisor each week.

---

## Receiving LOOP Central Deliveries

### Pre-Prepared / Cooked Items

Items arriving hot (cooked and held for delivery):
- Required receiving temperature: ≥65°C
- Temperature is checked with the probe thermometer on arrival
- If hot items arrive below 65°C, the Head Chef is notified — items are assessed for whether they can be reheated safely or must be discarded

Items arriving cold (cooked, cooled, and chilled for delivery):
- Required receiving temperature: ≤7°C
- Checked against the label — item name, prep date, use-by date must be present
- Treated identically to internally prepped items for FIFO and shelf-life purposes

### Semi-Prepared / Raw Items

- Marinated proteins, portioned raw items: ≤7°C on arrival
- Correct colour, smell, and physical condition checked — same quality standards as external supplier deliveries
- Label on arrival must include item name, prep/marinate date, and use-by date

---

## Handling Discrepancies with LOOP Central

If a LOOP Central delivery has missing items, incorrect items, or items that fail the receiving inspection:

1. Do not silently accept — document the issue immediately
2. Notify the Head Chef
3. Head Chef contacts the LOOP Central kitchen manager directly
4. Document: what was expected, what arrived, what was refused or noted

Because LOOP Central is an internal supplier, discrepancies are resolved through the Head Chef and LOOP Central kitchen manager — not through an external supplier complaint process.

---

## Integration into Service

LOOP Central items are stored and labelled using the same system as all other SPC items:
- Placed in the appropriate cold storage unit with FIFO applied
- Labels confirm origin (LOOP Central), item name, received date, and use-by date
- Used in the same rotation as internally prepped items of the same type
