---

## Purpose

To define the temperature logging requirement for all cold, frozen, and hot deliveries received at SPC.

---

## Why Temperature Logging Matters

Recording temperatures at the point of receiving creates an audit trail that protects SPC in the event of a food safety incident or inspection. It also helps identify unreliable suppliers — if a supplier consistently delivers above-temperature goods, the pattern is visible in the log.

---

## What Requires a Temperature Log Entry

Every delivery containing cold, frozen, or hot items gets a temperature log entry. Dry goods only deliveries (no refrigeration required) do not require a temperature entry, but the delivery date and items are still noted.

---

## Temperature Log — Per Delivery Entry

For each temperature-sensitive delivery, record:

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Date | Date of delivery |
| Supplier | Name of supplier or "LOOP Central" |
| Item(s) checked | Item name or category (e.g., "Chicken — raw", "Sauces — cooked") |
| Measured temperature | Temperature reading from probe thermometer |
| Required temperature | The required standard (≤7°C / ≤-18°C / ≥65°C) |
| Pass / Fail | Did the item meet the required temperature? |
| Action taken (if fail) | What was done — refused, accepted by Head Chef exception, discarded |
| Initials | Staff member who completed the check |

---

## Temperature Log Format

The log is maintained as a physical or digital record. The Head Chef decides the format (paper or tablet entry) and ensures a blank log is available at the receiving area.

A simple template:

```
Date: __________    Supplier: __________________________

Item             Measured Temp    Required Temp    Pass/Fail    Action
______________   _____________    _____________    _________    __________
______________   _____________    _____________    _________    __________

Received by: _______________ (initials)
```

---

## Retention

Temperature logs are retained for a minimum of 3 months. In the event of a food safety complaint or regulatory inspection, logs are produced on request.

---

## Probe Thermometer Care

- The probe is cleaned and sanitised between each use (between different items being tested)
- If the thermometer is giving inconsistent or clearly inaccurate readings, it is flagged to the Head Chef and not used until replaced or recalibrated
- A backup thermometer is available at the kitchen
