## Purpose

To define how incoming deliveries are received, inspected, and processed at SPC — ensuring all items are at the correct quality and temperature before entering the kitchen.

---

## Who Can Receive Deliveries

Deliveries are received by the Head Chef, Sous Chef, or a supervisor who has been trained in the receiving procedure. Untrained staff do not accept deliveries independently.

---

## Receiving Procedure

### Step 1 — Prepare the Receiving Area

Before the delivery arrives, prepare a clean receiving area:
- Clear the receiving bench or floor area of any existing items
- Have the expected delivery order on hand (purchase order or expected items list from the Head Chef)
- Have a thermometer available for temperature checks

### Step 2 — Count and Cross-Check

When the delivery arrives:
1. Count all items and check against the delivery note / invoice
2. Confirm item quantities match what was ordered
3. Note any short deliveries, substitutions, or items not ordered on the delivery note before signing

Do not sign the delivery note until the count is complete.

### Step 3 — Inspect Quality

For every delivery item:

| Item Type | Quality Check |
|---|---|
| Fresh produce | No visible mould, excessive bruising, or rot. Fresh smell. |
| Proteins (meat, poultry) | Correct colour for the cut. No off odour. Packaging intact. |
| Seafood | Firm texture. No strong off odour. Eyes clear (whole fish). |
| Dairy | Within use-by date. Packaging sealed and undamaged. |
| Dry goods | Packaging intact. No moisture damage. Within use-by date. |
| Frozen items | Solid frozen on arrival. No signs of partial thaw (ice crystals, soft texture). |

Any item that fails the quality check is refused at the door — it is not accepted into the kitchen pending Head Chef review.

### Step 4 — Temperature Verification

Cold and frozen items are temperature-checked using the kitchen probe thermometer:

| Item Type | Required Receiving Temperature |
|---|---|
| Fresh proteins, dairy, produce | ≤7°C |
| Frozen items | ≤-18°C (or solid frozen) |
| Hot-prepared items from LOOP Central | ≥65°C |

Items outside the required temperature range on arrival are refused unless the Head Chef makes a specific exception based on risk assessment (e.g., item was briefly in transit and condition is otherwise perfect).

### Step 5 — Storage

Once accepted, all items are put away immediately — nothing sits in the receiving area:
- Cold items go directly to the fridge
- Frozen items go directly to the freezer
- Dry goods go to the dry store
- FIFO is applied — newer stock to the back, older stock to the front

All items are labelled with the received date where they will be decanted or transferred to SPC containers.

### Step 6 — Sign the Delivery Note

After the count, quality check, and temperature check are complete and the delivery is accepted, sign the delivery note. Keep a copy for the Head Chef's records.

---

## Refusing a Delivery

If an item is refused, the receiving staff member:
1. Clearly marks the refused item on the delivery note — do not sign for it
2. Returns it to the driver — ensures it leaves the kitchen
3. Notifies the Head Chef
4. The Head Chef follows up with the supplier

---

## LOOP Central Deliveries

SPC receives regular deliveries from LOOP Central (央廚), the group production kitchen. See **SPC-DEL-002 — LOOP Central Delivery Handling** for the specific LOOP Central receiving procedure.
