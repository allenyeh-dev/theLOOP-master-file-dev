---

## Purpose

To define the correct shutdown sequence for all SPC kitchen equipment at the end of each shift. Correct shutdown sequence protects equipment, saves energy, and prevents gas or fire hazards overnight.

---

## Overview — Shutdown Order

Equipment is shut down in this order:

1. SINMAG ovens
2. Deep fryer (寶鼎牌)
3. Dishwasher
4. Water heater
5. Gas main valve
6. YAZAKI gas alarm
7. Ventilation & AC

Gas equipment (fryer) is shut down before the gas main is closed. Ventilation is always the last system off.

---

## Step 1 — SINMAG Ovens

Allow any remaining items to finish cooking before shutting down the ovens.

1. Power off all ovens using the power button
2. Leave oven doors slightly open to allow heat to dissipate — do not place anything on or near the open door
3. Confirm all oven displays are off

---

## Step 2 — Deep Fryer (寶鼎牌)

The fryer must be shut down before the gas main is closed.

![Deep fryer closing — turn the knob 180° and confirm through the flame window](../images/06-eqp/06-eqp_018.jpg)
*Caption: Deep fryer shutdown step 1 — turn the temperature/burner control knob 180° to the off position. After turning, look through the flame confirmation window to verify the burner has extinguished.*

![Deep fryer closing — step 2](../images/06-eqp/06-eqp_019.jpg)
*Caption: Fryer closing step 2 — confirm the pilot and main burner are both out. The flame confirmation window should show no visible flame before moving to the next step.*

**Deep fryer shutdown:**
1. Turn the temperature/burner control knob **180° to the off position**
2. Confirm through the flame confirmation window that the burner is extinguished
3. Allow the oil to cool — do not cover a hot fryer with a lid
4. Do not drain or change the oil until it has fully cooled

---

## Step 3 — Dishwasher

![Dishwasher closing](../images/06-eqp/06-eqp_022.jpg)
*Caption: Dishwasher closing — run a final cycle with no dishes if required to rinse the interior, then power off the unit.*

![Dishwasher auto-shutdown — confirm unit is off](../images/06-eqp/06-eqp_023.jpg)
*Caption: Dishwasher powered down. Leave the door slightly open to allow the interior to dry and air out overnight — a sealed, warm, wet dishwasher breeds bacteria and odours.*

**Dishwasher shutdown:**
1. Ensure all dishes from the last cycle have been cleared
2. Power off the unit
3. Leave the door **slightly open** to ventilate overnight

---

## Step 4 — Water Heater

![Water heater closing — step 1](../images/06-eqp/06-eqp_020.jpg)
*Caption: Water heater closing step 1 — power off the unit using the control button.*

![Water heater closing — step 2](../images/06-eqp/06-eqp_021.jpg)
*Caption: Water heater closing step 2 — close the gas valve at the water heater unit (handle perpendicular to pipe = closed).*

**Water heater shutdown — 2 steps:**
1. Power off the unit
2. Close the gas valve at the water heater unit (handle **perpendicular** to pipe = closed)

---

## Step 5 — Gas Main Valve

With all gas appliances confirmed off, close the gas main valve.

![Gas valve in open position — parallel to pipe](../images/06-eqp/06-eqp_024.png)
*Caption: Gas valve currently in the OPEN position — handle parallel to the pipe. This is the starting position at closing.*

![Gas valve moved to closed position — perpendicular to pipe](../images/06-eqp/06-eqp_025.png)
*Caption: Gas valve in the CLOSED position — handle rotated to perpendicular (vertical / 90°) to the pipe. This is the correct overnight position. Confirm the handle is fully perpendicular before leaving.*

**Action:** Rotate the gas main valve handle from **parallel (open)** to **perpendicular (closed)**.

Do not close the gas main while any gas appliance is still burning.

---

## Step 6 — YAZAKI Gas Alarm

After the gas main is closed, the YAZAKI alarm panel is checked and secured.

![YAZAKI gas alarm — closing button](../images/06-eqp/06-eqp_026.jpg)
*Caption: The YAZAKI alarm closing procedure — confirm no alarm is active, then press the designated button to confirm the system has been checked. If an alarm is showing, do not close the system — follow SPC-EQP-004 first.*

**Action:** Confirm the YAZAKI panel shows normal status (green) and complete the closing confirmation on the panel.

---

## Step 7 — Ventilation & AC

Ventilation is the last system shut down — it must remain running until all gas equipment has been confirmed off and the kitchen is clear.

![Ventilation closing — green 停止 (stop) button on panel](../images/06-eqp/06-eqp_027.jpg)
*Caption: Ventilation closing — press the green 停止 (stop) button on the ventilation panel to shut down the system.*

![Ventilation closing — confirm system has stopped](../images/06-eqp/06-eqp_028.jpg)
*Caption: Confirm the ventilation fan has stopped. Airflow should cease and the display (if present) should show the system is off.*

**Action:** Press the **綠色 停止 (green Stop) button** on the ventilation panel. Confirm the fan has stopped before leaving.

---

## Closing Confirmation Checklist

Before the closing supervisor leaves the kitchen:

- [ ] All SINMAG ovens powered off — doors open to cool
- [ ] Deep fryer burner extinguished — flame window confirmed clear
- [ ] Dishwasher powered off — door left open
- [ ] Water heater powered off and gas valve at unit closed
- [ ] Gas main valve confirmed **perpendicular (closed)**
- [ ] YAZAKI alarm panel — normal status confirmed
- [ ] Ventilation confirmed off
- [ ] Kitchen lights off
- [ ] All cold storage doors closed
- [ ] Kitchen door locked
