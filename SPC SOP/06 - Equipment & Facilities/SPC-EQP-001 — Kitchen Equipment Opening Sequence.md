# SPC-EQP-001 — Kitchen Equipment Opening Sequence

| **Effective Date** | June 2026 |

| **Owner** | Head Chef |

---

## Purpose

To define the correct startup sequence for all SPC kitchen equipment. The sequence must be followed in order — each step enables the next. No step is skipped.

---

## Overview — Startup Order

Equipment is started in this order:

1. Ventilation & AC
2. Gas main valve
3. YAZAKI gas alarm — confirm normal status
4. Water heater
5. Dishwasher
6. Deep fryer (寶鼎牌)
7. SINMAG ovens

---

## Step 1 — Ventilation & AC

The ventilation system must be running before any gas equipment is turned on.

![Ventilation and AC panel — red 運轉 (run) button circled in red](../images/06-eqp/06-eqp_001.jpg)
*Caption: The ventilation/AC control panel. Press the red 運轉 (run) button to start the ventilation system. Confirm the system is running before proceeding.*

![Second ventilation panel — confirm running status](../images/06-eqp/06-eqp_002.jpg)
*Caption: Confirm ventilation is actively running. Do not proceed to gas equipment until the fan is audibly running and confirmed on.*

**Action:** Press the **紅色 運轉 (red Run) button** on the ventilation panel. Confirm airflow before proceeding.

---

## Step 2 — Gas Main Valve

With ventilation confirmed running, open the gas main valve.

![Gas valve in closed position — handle perpendicular (vertical) to the pipe](../images/06-eqp/06-eqp_003.png)
*Caption: Gas valve in the CLOSED position. The handle sits perpendicular (vertical / at 90°) to the gas pipe direction. This is the safe, off-position.*

![Gas valve in open position — handle parallel (horizontal) to the pipe](../images/06-eqp/06-eqp_004.png)
*Caption: Gas valve in the OPEN position. The handle is parallel (horizontal / aligned) with the gas pipe direction. Turn the valve from vertical to horizontal to open gas supply to the kitchen.*

**Action:** Rotate the gas valve handle from **perpendicular (closed)** to **parallel (open)** with the pipe.

---

## Step 3 — YAZAKI Gas Cut-Off Alarm

Before proceeding to any gas appliance, check the YAZAKI gas alarm panel.

![YAZAKI gas cut-off alarm panel — green indicator light and error reference table](../images/06-eqp/06-eqp_005.jpg)
*Caption: The YAZAKI gas cut-off alarm panel. In normal operation, the green light is on and no error codes are displayed. If the red alarm light is showing or an error code is present, do not proceed — follow the alarm response procedure in SPC-EQP-004.*

**Action:** Confirm the YAZAKI panel shows **green (normal)** status. If any alarm or error code is showing, follow **SPC-EQP-004 — YAZAKI Gas Cut-Off Alarm Procedure** before proceeding.

---

## Step 4 — Water Heater

The water heater requires gas supply to be open (Step 2) before startup.

![Water heater — power socket circled in red](../images/06-eqp/06-eqp_006.jpg)
*Caption: Step 1 of water heater startup — confirm the power socket for the water heater is switched on and plugged in.*

![Water heater — gas valve at the unit](../images/06-eqp/06-eqp_007.jpg)
*Caption: Step 2 — open the gas valve at the water heater unit itself (parallel to pipe = open).*

![Water heater — blue power button pressed to ignite](../images/06-eqp/06-eqp_008.jpg)
*Caption: Step 3 — press the blue power/ignition button on the water heater to start the unit. Confirm the pilot or burner has lit.*

**Water heater startup — 3 steps:**
1. Confirm power socket is switched on
2. Open the gas valve at the water heater unit (handle parallel to pipe)
3. Press the **blue power/ignition button** — confirm the unit has lit

---

## Step 5 — Dishwasher

![Dishwasher opening step — door and startup](../images/06-eqp/06-eqp_009.jpg)
*Caption: Dishwasher startup — open the door to confirm the water level inside is correct, then close and power on.*

![Dishwasher green indicator light — unit ready](../images/06-eqp/06-eqp_010.jpg)
*Caption: The green indicator light confirms the dishwasher has completed its startup cycle and is ready for use. Do not load dishes until the green light is showing.*

**Dishwasher startup:**
1. Open the door — confirm water level is correct
2. Close the door and power on the unit
3. Wait for the **green ready indicator light** before loading

---

## Step 6 — Deep Fryer (寶鼎牌)

The fryer uses gas. Gas supply (Step 2) must be confirmed open before lighting the pilot.

![Deep fryer — gas pipe connection and red arrow indicating the gas feed](../images/06-eqp/06-eqp_011.jpg)
*Caption: The gas pipe connection to the deep fryer. Confirm the gas line to the fryer is clear and connected before attempting to light.*

![Deep fryer — pilot light procedure](../images/06-eqp/06-eqp_012.jpg)
*Caption: Lighting the pilot on the 寶鼎牌 fryer. Follow the manufacturer's pilot lighting procedure — hold the ignition button and wait for the pilot flame to catch before releasing.*

![Deep fryer — temperature control knob turned to desired setting](../images/06-eqp/06-eqp_013.jpg)
*Caption: Once the pilot is lit and confirmed stable, turn the main burner knob to the required temperature setting. Allow the oil to reach the correct frying temperature before service.*

**Deep fryer startup — 3 steps:**
1. Confirm gas line to the fryer is connected and main gas is open
2. Light the pilot flame — hold ignition button until pilot catches and holds
3. Turn the temperature control knob to the required service setting

Allow the oil to reach the required frying temperature before service begins. Do not attempt to fry before the oil is at temperature.

---

## Step 7 — SINMAG Ovens

The SPC kitchen uses SINMAG ovens. Three ovens are available. Opening procedure uses dough baking programs first, then service programs.

![SINMAG oven — power button circled, finger pressing the button](../images/06-eqp/06-eqp_014.jpg)
*Caption: SINMAG oven startup — press the power button (circled) to turn on the oven. The display will illuminate.*

![SINMAG oven — program selection screen](../images/06-eqp/06-eqp_015.jpg)
*Caption: Program selection. For morning dough baking, select Program 10 (290°C upper / 275°C lower). For service (pizza), switch to Program 11 (235°C upper / 275°C lower).*

![SINMAG oven — temperature display confirmed](../images/06-eqp/06-eqp_016.jpg)
*Caption: Temperature display confirmed. Wait for the oven to reach the set temperature before loading. The display shows current and target temperature.*

![SINMAG oven — door closed and unit running](../images/06-eqp/06-eqp_017.jpg)
*Caption: Oven door closed with the unit heating up. Do not open the door repeatedly while the oven is pre-heating — this extends warm-up time significantly.*

**SINMAG oven programs:**

| Program | Use | Upper Temp | Lower Temp |
|---|---|---|---|
| Program 10 | Dough baking (morning) | 290°C | 275°C |
| Program 11 | Pizza (service) | 235°C | 275°C |

**Opening startup sequence:**
1. Power on all three ovens — press the power button on each
2. Select **Program 10** on all three ovens for dough baking
3. Allow ovens to pre-heat before loading dough
4. Once dough baking is complete, shut down one oven
5. Switch remaining two ovens to **Program 11** for service

---

## Opening Complete

Once all equipment is running, the supervisor confirms:
- [ ] Ventilation audibly running
- [ ] Gas main valve open
- [ ] YAZAKI alarm showing green
- [ ] Water heater lit and producing hot water
- [ ] Dishwasher at green ready status
- [ ] Fryer oil at temperature
- [ ] Ovens at correct program temperature

Kitchen is ready for prep.
