## Purpose

To define the packaging and handover standards for all SPC delivery orders — ensuring food arrives at the customer in the correct condition, at the right temperature, and presented to SPC's standard.

---

## Packaging Principles

SPC food goes out under the brand name and reputation of the kitchen. The packaging is the customer's first physical contact with SPC. Every order that leaves the kitchen reflects on the brand.

- Food is packaged while hot — packaging happens immediately when the item is plated, not left to cool on a bench first
- Hot and cold items in the same order are packaged in separate containers where required — they are not combined into one container where this would compromise temperature or quality
- Soup, sauce, and liquid items are sealed to prevent spill in transit
- Fragile or presentation-sensitive items (e.g., salads, assembled dishes) are packed in a way that minimises movement and tipping

---

## Packaging Checklist

For every outgoing order:

- [ ] All items in the order are present — count against the platform order ticket
- [ ] Hot items are hot at the time of packaging
- [ ] Portions are correct — not under-portioned
- [ ] Any modifiers or special requests are actioned (extra sauce, no onion, etc.)
- [ ] Sauces and liquid items are sealed securely
- [ ] Bag is sealed or closed — the seal or staple protects against tampering during delivery

---

## Food Photography Standard

For new items or menu quality monitoring, the supervisor photographs finished dishes before packaging. This visual record is used for:
- Quality consistency checking
- Comparison against the Head Chef's plating standard
- Evidence in the event of a customer presentation complaint

## Handover to Rider

When a delivery rider collects an order:
1. Confirm the order number on the bag matches what the rider is collecting (platform confirmation screen)
2. Hand over the sealed bag — do not open or unseal
3. If the platform requires any confirmation step (e.g., scanning, digital confirmation), complete it before the rider leaves
4. If the rider arrives and the order is not yet ready, give an estimated wait time — do not rush and compromise quality

---

## Rider Conduct at SPC

Riders are not permitted in the food preparation area. Collections happen at the designated collection point (window, counter, or designated handover area). Staff do not allow riders to wander into the kitchen.

---

## Packaging Materials

Packaging materials (containers, bags, seals, labels) are stocked as part of the SPC dry inventory. The supervisor flags low packaging stock during the daily inventory check (SPC-INV-001). Running out of packaging materials mid-service is an operational failure — it is managed proactively.
