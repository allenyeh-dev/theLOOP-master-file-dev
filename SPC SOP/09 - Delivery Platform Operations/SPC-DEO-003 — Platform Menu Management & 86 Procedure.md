## Purpose

To define how SPC manages menu availability on delivery platforms — including how items are 86'd on the platform, how platform menus are updated, and how pricing or item changes are coordinated.

---

## Platform Menu vs. Kitchen Menu

The delivery platform menu must always reflect what the kitchen can actually prepare and serve. A customer who orders an unavailable item and receives a cancellation or substitution without warning has a bad experience. The platform menu is kept accurate at all times.

---

## 86ing an Item on the Platform

When an item is 86'd in the kitchen, it must also be removed from the platform immediately.

**When to 86 on the platform:**
- An ingredient required for the item has run out
- The item has been temporarily suspended by the Head Chef
- The kitchen is at capacity and the item cannot be prepared within a reasonable prep time

**How to 86 on the platform:**
1. Access the platform management dashboard on the tablet/device
2. Navigate to the menu or item availability section
3. Mark the item as **unavailable / sold out**
4. Confirm the change has taken effect (the item no longer appears as orderable to customers)

For the full kitchen 86 procedure, see **SPC-OPS-003 — 86 Item Procedure**.

**Re-opening an 86'd item:**
When the item is available again (ingredient restocked, kitchen capacity restored), it is re-enabled on the platform. Confirm the item is fully prep-ready before re-enabling.

---

## Platform Opening & Closing

**Opening:** The supervisor confirms the platform is set to **OPEN** and all available items are enabled before the first order can come in. See **SPC-DEO-001**.

**Closing:** At the end of service, the platform is set to **CLOSED** before the kitchen stops accepting orders. Do not leave the platform open while the kitchen is winding down service — this results in orders arriving that cannot be fulfilled.

**Last order cutoff:** The supervisor sets the platform to closed or pauses new orders a reasonable time before the kitchen closes — the Head Chef sets the cutoff time based on prep time for the most complex menu item.

---

## Menu Updates

Menu updates (new items, price changes, item removals) are made by the Head Chef or a designated staff member with platform admin access.

**Adding a new item:**
- Item is live on the platform only after the Head Chef has confirmed it is production-ready
- Photos and descriptions on the platform match the actual dish
- Pricing is confirmed before the item goes live

**Removing an item:**
- Permanent removal is different from an 86 — the item is fully deleted from the platform menu, not just marked unavailable
- Head Chef authorises all permanent menu changes

**Price changes:**
- Price changes are approved by the Head Chef and F&B Director
- Prices are updated on the platform at the same time as any in-kitchen or internal price communication

---

## Platform Access

Platform login credentials are managed by the Head Chef. Staff with operational access (to manage availability and 86s during service) are trained on the platform tablet by the Head Chef or Sous Chef before they are given access.
