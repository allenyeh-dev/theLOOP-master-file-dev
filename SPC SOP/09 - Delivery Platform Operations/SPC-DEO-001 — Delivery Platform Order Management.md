---

## Purpose

SPC is a ghost kitchen. All customer orders arrive through delivery platforms (e.g., Foodpanda, Uber Eats, or equivalent). This SOP defines how orders are received, managed, and dispatched.

---

## Platform Setup at Opening

Before service begins, the supervisor confirms the delivery platform tablet or device is:
- [ ] Powered on and connected to the network
- [ ] Platform app is running and logged in
- [ ] Store status is set to **OPEN** — the store is not left closed after opening
- [ ] Any items that are 86'd are marked as unavailable on the platform (see SPC-DEO-003)
- [ ] Prep time settings reflect current kitchen capacity

---

## Order Flow

1. **Order arrives** — the platform device alerts. The kitchen acknowledges the order within the platform's required response window (typically 1–2 minutes)
2. **Order printed or displayed** — confirm the order ticket or screen shows the full order
3. **Kitchen prepares the order** — follow the same no-ticket, no-food rule as dine-in (SPC-OPS-004)
4. **Order assembled and packaged** — follow **SPC-DEO-002 — Packaging & Handover Standards**
5. **Order marked ready on platform** — update the platform status so the rider is notified to collect
6. **Rider collects** — verify the order against the platform ticket before handover if the platform requires it

---

## Order Priority

When multiple orders are active simultaneously:
- Orders are prepared in the order they were received unless a later order has a shorter prep time (smaller order)
- The supervisor manages queue flow during busy periods
- If the kitchen is overwhelmed, temporarily pause the platform (reduce or pause accepting new orders) rather than accepting orders that cannot be delivered on time

**A late or wrong order arriving at a customer is worse than a brief platform pause.**

---

## Order Accuracy

Before packaging any order:
- Read the full order — check for modifiers, special requests, or exclusions
- If an order has a note from the customer, it is read and actioned — not ignored
- Any special request that cannot be accommodated (e.g., allergen modification the kitchen cannot safely fulfil) is immediately reported to the supervisor, who contacts the customer through the platform before preparing the food

---

## Order Errors

If a wrong item is packaged and the rider has not yet collected:
- Replace the item immediately — correct the order before handover
- Do not send a wrong order hoping the customer won't notice

If a wrong order is only discovered after rider collection:
- Notify the supervisor
- The supervisor handles the customer complaint through the platform (SPC-OPS-005)

---

## Platform Performance

The Head Chef monitors platform metrics: order acceptance rate, prep time, cancellations, and customer ratings. Consistent performance issues are reviewed with staff and addressed operationally.
