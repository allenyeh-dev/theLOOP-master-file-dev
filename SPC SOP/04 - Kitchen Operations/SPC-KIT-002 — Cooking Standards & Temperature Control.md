## Purpose

To define the cooking temperature standards at SPC — ensuring all food is cooked, held, and served at safe temperatures to prevent foodborne illness.

---

## Critical Temperature Reference

| Situation | Required Temperature |
|---|---|
| Cold storage (fridge) | ≤7°C |
| Frozen storage | ≤-18°C |
| Hot-held food (ready to serve) | ≥65°C |
| Cold food (ready to serve) | ≤7°C |
| Cooked food centre temperature | ≥70°C |
| Sashimi / raw fish | Refrigerated ≤7°C at all times |
| Danger zone (do not leave food in this range) | 7°C – 65°C |

---

## Cooking Requirements

**All cooked items:**
The centre temperature of all cooked food must reach at least 70°C before serving. For thick cuts of meat, whole poultry, or dense items, use a probe thermometer to verify the centre temperature. Do not estimate by appearance alone.

**Raw fish / sashimi:**
Raw fish must be kept refrigerated at ≤7°C at all times. It is never left at room temperature during service. If it cannot be maintained cold during a busy service period, it is stored in a cold insert or returned to refrigeration between orders.

**Time limits:**
Cooked food held at serving temperature (≥65°C) must be consumed or disposed of within 2 hours. Food that has been sitting for more than 2 hours without being kept at the correct temperature is not served and is discarded.

---

## Oven Programs — SPC Kitchen

The SPC kitchen uses SINMAG ovens. The standard programs are:

| Program | Use | Upper Temp | Lower Temp |
|---|---|---|---|
| Program 10 | Dough baking | 290°C | 275°C |
| Program 11 | Pizza (service) | 235°C | 275°C |

After dough baking is complete at the start of service, one oven is shut down and the remaining two ovens are switched to Program 11 for service. See **SPC-EQP-001** for oven startup procedure.

---

## Temperature Log

A temperature log is completed at the start and end of every shift and at the required interval during service (set by Head Chef — typically every 2–4 hours):

| Log Entry | When |
|---|---|
| Cold storage opening check | Shift start |
| Freezer opening check | Shift start |
| Mid-service cold storage check | As scheduled |
| Closing cold storage check | Shift end |

If any reading is outside the required range, the supervisor is notified immediately. Food safety action is taken before service continues from that unit.

---

## Probe Thermometer

- A calibrated probe thermometer is available at the kitchen at all times
- The thermometer is cleaned and sanitised between uses
- If the thermometer is lost, damaged, or giving unreliable readings, the supervisor sources a replacement before service continues
