## Purpose

To define the prep and mise en place standards at SPC — ensuring every station is fully set up before service begins and that all prepped items are stored, labelled, and ready to use.

---

## General Mise en Place Principles

- All prep is completed before service begins. No station is considered ready until all mise en place is in place
- All prepped items are stored in the correct container, covered, labelled with item name and prep date, and stored at the correct temperature
- Workbenches are clean and dry before prep begins
- Tools and utensils needed for the session are assembled before starting

---

## Station Prep Checklist — All Stations

At the start of each shift, each station completes the following:

**Surfaces & tools:**
- [ ] Workbench wiped and dry
- [ ] Correct cutting board colour for station (see SPC-FSH-005)
- [ ] Knives, utensils, and tools clean and in place
- [ ] Gloves available at station

**Cold prep & storage:**
- [ ] Cold storage items checked — verify temperature is within range (≤7°C)
- [ ] Items labelled — check all prep containers have a date label
- [ ] Items within their shelf life — flag any items nearing expiry to supervisor
- [ ] FIFO applied — older stock at the front, newer stock at the back

**Station-specific prep:**
- [ ] All mise en place required for the shift's menu is prepped and in place
- [ ] Portion sizes are correct and consistent with Head Chef's standards
- [ ] Any sauces, bases, or batches that need to be made fresh are made and tasted before service

---

## Labelling Standards

All prepped items must be labelled before being stored. Every label must include:

- Item name
- Prep date (and time if applicable)
- Use-by date / defrost date if applicable
- Staff member's initials (optional but recommended)

Labels must be legible and attached directly to the container — not the lid.

---

## Prep Communication

If prep cannot be completed before service due to delivery delays, staffing issues, or equipment faults:

- Supervisor is notified immediately
- Affected items are 86'd until prep is complete (SPC-OPS-003)
- Head Chef is notified if the issue will materially affect service

---

## End-of-Prep Check

Before the supervisor opens service, a walkthrough of all stations confirms:

- [ ] All stations are fully prepped and ready
- [ ] All items correctly labelled and stored
- [ ] No items left uncovered or unlabelled on workbenches
- [ ] Cold storage closed and at correct temperature
- [ ] All stations signed off ready
