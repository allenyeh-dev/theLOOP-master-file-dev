## Purpose

To define the cleaning and sanitation standards that keep the SPC kitchen safe, compliant, and ready for inspection at any time.

---

## General Principles

- The kitchen is cleaned continuously during service — not only at the end of the shift
- No workbench is left dirty while in use — clean as you go
- No standing water on workbenches or floors
- 75% alcohol is used for surface disinfection where required

---

## During-Service Cleaning

- [ ] Workbenches wiped clean between different tasks — especially when switching between raw and cooked items
- [ ] Spills on the floor addressed immediately — slip hazard
- [ ] All used utensils returned to the dishwasher promptly — not left on benches
- [ ] Waste disposed of into covered bins — not left on prep surfaces

---

## End-of-Shift Cleaning Checklist

**Workbenches:**
- [ ] All surfaces wiped down with clean cloth and cleaning agent
- [ ] No food debris, moisture, or grease remaining on any bench surface
- [ ] Disinfect with 75% alcohol where required by the Head Chef's protocol

**Floor:**
- [ ] Swept to remove all food debris
- [ ] Mopped with cleaning agent — no standing water
- [ ] Drains cleared and rinsed — drains are a primary source of bacterial growth and must be cleaned daily

**Cutting boards:**
- [ ] All cutting boards run through the dishwasher at the end of the shift
- [ ] Dishwasher high-temperature cycle used for sanitisation
- [ ] Boards stored dry — never stored wet or stacked together while damp

**Utensils & equipment surfaces:**
- [ ] All prep utensils run through the dishwasher
- [ ] Equipment exterior surfaces wiped down (oven doors, fryer exterior, dishwasher exterior)
- [ ] Refrigerator door handles and exterior wiped down

**Waste:**
- [ ] All waste bins emptied at end of shift
- [ ] Bin liners replaced
- [ ] Waste area around bins swept and mopped

---

## Weekly Deep Clean

The Head Chef schedules a weekly deep clean covering:
- Inside of all cold storage units — shelves removed and cleaned
- Behind and under all equipment
- Exhaust filters (if applicable within SPC's maintenance scope)
- Any areas not covered in the daily routine

---

## Pest Control

- Any signs of pest activity (droppings, gnaw marks, live insects) are reported to the Head Chef immediately
- The kitchen maintains a pest control schedule coordinated with the building/property manager
- No food is stored directly on the floor (minimum 15cm off ground on shelving)
- All dry goods are stored in sealed containers
