## Purpose

To define how performance issues and conduct violations are addressed at SPC — fairly, consistently, and in line with Taiwan Labour Standards Act requirements.

---

## Performance Management

Performance concerns are addressed through direct conversation first. The Head Chef is responsible for addressing performance issues promptly — issues that are not addressed tend to escalate.

**Step 1 — Verbal coaching:**
The Head Chef or supervisor speaks directly with the staff member. The conversation covers what the issue is, what the expected standard is, and what improvement is needed. This is logged informally in the supervisor's notes.

**Step 2 — Formal verbal warning:**
If the issue continues after coaching, the Head Chef issues a formal verbal warning. This is documented in writing — date, issue, expectation, and consequence if unresolved — and filed in the staff member's record. Ownership and LOOP Central HR are notified.

**Step 3 — Written warning:**
If the issue continues, a written warning is issued. The staff member signs to acknowledge receipt. Filed in HR records with Ownership and LOOP Central HR copied.

**Step 4 — Further action:**
If improvement does not occur after a written warning, the Head Chef consults with Ownership and LOOP Central HR on next steps — which may include a performance improvement plan or termination per Taiwan Labour Standards Act.

---

## Conduct Violations

Some conduct violations may bypass the coaching stage and proceed directly to formal warning or termination depending on severity. These include:

- Deliberate food safety violations (e.g., serving expired food knowingly)
- Serving food without a ticket and concealing it
- Theft
- Violence or harassment in the workplace
- Falsifying records

The Head Chef consults Ownership before taking formal action on any of the above.

---

## Staff Termination

All terminations follow Taiwan Labour Standards Act requirements:
- Advance notice period or payment in lieu, as required by law
- Final pay including any outstanding entitlements
- LOOP Central HR coordinates all statutory filings

The Head Chef does not terminate any staff member without prior alignment with Ownership.
