## Purpose

To define how SPC recruits, hires, and onboards new kitchen staff — ensuring every new team member is properly set up, trained, and compliant before working an independent shift.

---

## Roles at SPC

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Head Chef | Overall kitchen leadership, menu, food safety accountability |
| Sous Chef / Supervisor | Shift management, equipment, handover, staff oversight |
| Line Cook | Station prep, cooking, order execution |
| Prep Cook | Mise en place, receiving support, cleaning |

---

## Hiring Process

**Step 1 —** Head Chef identifies a staffing need and notifies ownership/HR.

**Step 2 —** Candidate is interviewed by the Head Chef. Key criteria: food safety awareness, ability to work under pressure, reliability, and willingness to follow systems.

**Step 3 —** Successful candidate completes:
- Employment contract
- Labour insurance and health insurance enrollment (coordinated with LOOP Central HR)
- Labour pension enrollment

**Step 4 —** Head Chef creates the staff account on The Loop portal before the first shift.

---

## New Hire Onboarding Checklist

All new hires complete the following before working independently:

**Day 1 — Orientation:**
- [ ] Kitchen tour — all stations, storage areas, equipment locations
- [ ] Introduction to the team and shift structure
- [ ] Platform Architecture and role access briefed
- [ ] Uniform issued and standards explained
- [ ] Emergency procedures briefed (fire exits, evacuation, first aid kit location)

**Days 1–3 — Supervised training:**
- [ ] Food safety rules reviewed and signed off (SPC-FSH-001 through SPC-FSH-005)
- [ ] Equipment opening and closing observed and practised (SPC-EQP-001 / SPC-EQP-002)
- [ ] Order flow and in-service standards reviewed (SPC-OPS-004)
- [ ] 86 procedure practised (SPC-OPS-003)
- [ ] Station-specific prep standards reviewed with Head Chef

**Before independent work:**
- [ ] Food safety quiz completed and passed
- [ ] Equipment startup/shutdown completed independently under observation
- [ ] Supervisor sign-off on all above items

---

## Probation Period

All new staff serve a 90-day probation period. During probation:

- Performance is reviewed at 30 and 60 days by the Head Chef
- Any concerns are documented and communicated to the staff member directly
- At 90 days, the Head Chef completes a probation assessment and recommends pass, extension, or termination to ownership/HR

---

## Staff Records

The Head Chef maintains a record for every team member including:
- Contract and employment documents
- Training completion records
- Food safety certification (if applicable)
- Performance review records
- Any disciplinary records

Records are stored per LOOP Central HR guidelines.
