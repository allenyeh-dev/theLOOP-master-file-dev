## Purpose

To define how shifts are scheduled, communicated, and managed at SPC — ensuring adequate coverage at all times and clear expectations for all staff.

---

## Shift Structure

SPC operates in shifts aligned to KOR's service hours and delivery platform hours. The typical shift structure:

| Shift | Hours | Role Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| Opening / Day | Morning → late afternoon | Supervisor + cook(s) |
| Evening | Afternoon → close | Supervisor + cook(s) |
| Late night (大夜) | Late evening → close | Supervisor + cook(s) |

Exact shift times are set by the Head Chef based on service volume and are communicated via the weekly schedule.

---

## Schedule Publication

- The Head Chef publishes the weekly schedule at least 3 days before the week begins
- The schedule is posted to the staff communication channel and/or The Loop portal
- Staff are expected to confirm receipt and flag any conflicts within 24 hours of publication

---

## Absence & Leave

**Planned leave:**
- Staff submit leave requests in advance via the designated channel or The Loop portal
- The Head Chef approves or declines based on coverage requirements
- Approved leave is noted in the schedule before it is published

**Unplanned absence:**
- Staff must notify their supervisor as early as possible before their shift — not after
- The supervisor assesses coverage and contacts the Head Chef if a replacement is needed
- All absences are noted in the shift handover for that day

---

## Lateness

- Staff arriving late must notify the supervisor immediately — before the shift starts if possible
- Lateness is noted in the shift handover note by the supervisor
- Repeated lateness is addressed through the performance management process (SPC-HR-004)

---

## Overtime

- Overtime is authorised by the supervisor on shift and notified to the Head Chef
- Overtime hours are recorded in the shift handover note
- Compensation follows LOOP Central HR guidelines and Taiwan Labour Standards Act

---

## Minimum Staffing Requirement

A shift may not operate below minimum staffing levels. Minimum coverage:

- At least one qualified supervisor on shift at all times
- At least one cook capable of running the line independently

If minimum coverage cannot be met, the Head Chef is notified immediately and a plan is made before service begins — which may include delaying service start, reducing menu availability, or calling in additional staff.
