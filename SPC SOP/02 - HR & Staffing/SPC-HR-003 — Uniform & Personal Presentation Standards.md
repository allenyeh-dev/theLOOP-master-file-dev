## Purpose

To define the required uniform and personal presentation standards for all SPC kitchen staff — ensuring food safety compliance and a consistent, professional appearance.

---

## Required Uniform

All kitchen staff must wear the full uniform every shift:

| Item | Standard |
|---|---|
| Chef coat | White |
| Trousers | Black cotton work trousers |
| Cap | Black |
| Apron | Black |
| Footwear | Non-slip kitchen shoes |

No substitutions. Staff who arrive without the correct uniform are sent to change before starting work.

---

## Personal Hygiene Standards

**Hands:**
- Washed correctly on arrival, before handling food, after using the toilet, and whenever hands may be contaminated (see SPC-FSH-001)
- No watches, rings, bracelets, or any jewellery on hands or wrists
- Nails kept short — nails must not be visible when looking at the palm side of the hand
- Any cuts or open wounds on the hands must be covered with a waterproof plaster and a disposable glove before handling food

**Hair:**
- Hair fully covered by the cap
- No loose strands visible
- Long hair tied up before the cap is worn

**General:**
- No strong cologne or perfume — can affect food
- No fake nails or nail polish
- Staff who are unwell (vomiting, diarrhoea, open infected wounds) must not handle food and must notify the supervisor immediately

---

## Uniform Maintenance

- Staff are responsible for keeping their uniform clean and presentable
- Worn, stained, or damaged uniform items must be reported to the supervisor for replacement
- Uniform is changed if it becomes heavily soiled during a shift — spare items should be available

---

## Supervisor Check

The opening supervisor checks the team's uniform and personal presentation at the start of every shift before food handling begins. Any staff not meeting the standard are corrected before work begins.
