---

## Purpose

To define the conditions under which fresh ingredients may be used beyond their standard shelf life — and to establish clear rules around who can authorise an extension, how it is documented, and what happens when extension is not possible.

---

## Definition — Fresh Ingredients

For the purposes of this policy, "fresh ingredients" means:

- Raw meat and poultry that has been defrosted
- Refrigerated processed items (not frozen)
- Items purchased for resale that are stored under refrigeration

This policy does not apply to dry goods, frozen items that have not been defrosted, or items from LOOP Central that are still sealed and within their labelled date.

---

## Standard Rule

All fresh ingredients must be used within their labelled shelf life or by the date assigned at the time of defrosting or preparation. When in doubt, discard.

---

## Extension — Conditions

An extension may be considered only if:

- The ingredient is within its labelled date but has not sold at the expected rate
- The ingredient is physically inspected by the Head Chef or Sous Chef and is in good condition (correct colour, smell, texture — no signs of spoilage)
- The extension does not cause the item to be used past a point where quality or safety could be compromised

**Extensions are never granted by kitchen staff.** Only the Head Chef or Sous Chef may decide to extend an ingredient.

---

## Extension — Procedure

**Step 1 —** Head Chef or Sous Chef physically inspects the ingredient.

**Step 2 —** If the ingredient passes inspection, the extension is authorised. A new label is applied immediately:

- Item name
- Original date / defrost date
- Extended use-by date (maximum: original date + 1 day)
- Name of the person authorising the extension

**Step 3 —** The extended item is re-checked at the start of the next shift. If it is still unsold and a second extension is being considered, the Head Chef makes the call — the item may not be extended a second time without Head Chef sign-off.

**Step 4 —** If the extended item is not used by the extended date, it is discarded. No further extensions.

---

## When Extension Is Not Possible

If the ingredient cannot be extended (failed inspection, already at maximum extension, or Head Chef declines):

**Option A — Staff meal:** The ingredient may be repurposed as a staff meal if it is still safe to consume.

**Option B — Disposal:** The ingredient is discarded. A disposal form is completed with the item name, quantity, and reason.

No ingredient is ever served to a guest that has failed inspection or exceeded its extension limit.

---

## Disposal Form

All disposed ingredients — whether expired or failed extension — must be logged on the disposal form before discarding. The form records:

- Date
- Item name
- Quantity
- Reason for disposal (expired / failed inspection / extended but unused)
- Staff member completing the disposal
- Supervisor sign-off
