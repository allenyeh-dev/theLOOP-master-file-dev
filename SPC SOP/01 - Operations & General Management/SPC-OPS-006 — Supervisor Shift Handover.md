# SPC-OPS-006 — Supervisor Shift Handover

| Field | Detail |

| **Effective Date** | June 2026 |
| **Version** | 1.0 |
| **Owner** | Head Chef / Kitchen Manager |

---

## Purpose

To ensure every shift handover is complete, accurate, and posted to the supervisor group before the outgoing supervisor leaves — so the incoming shift has everything they need to start without gaps.

---

## Handover Requirements

The outgoing supervisor must complete and post the handover note to the supervisor group before leaving. The incoming supervisor must read and acknowledge it before the shift begins.

No supervisor may leave until the handover note is posted.

---

## Handover Note — Required Items

Every shift handover note must include all of the following:

**1. 86 items & unopened items**
List any items that are currently closed. For each:
- Item name
- Reason it is closed (sold out, ingredient issue, quality issue)
- Expected resolution (e.g., LOOP Central delivery expected tomorrow morning)

**2. Items to push**
List any items that should be promoted by the incoming shift — typically items nearing their use-by date or items that are slow-selling and building up stock.

**3. Supplier issues & outstanding deliveries**
Any items that did not arrive as expected, or any supplier issues that need follow-up. Include what action has already been taken.

**4. Low stock alerts**
Any ingredients that are critically low and may become an issue during the incoming shift. Incoming supervisor should check these first.

**5. Pre-ordered items for the next day**
List any items that have already been pre-ordered or pre-confirmed for the following day's service. Include what has been confirmed and what still needs confirmation.

**6. Key announcements / notes to communicate**
Any procedures, policy updates, or operational reminders that need to be communicated to the incoming kitchen team.

**7. Staff notes**
Any relevant HR or staffing information: absences, late arrivals, overtime worked, any follow-up actions required.

---

## Handover Note Format

The handover note is written in Traditional Chinese and posted to the supervisor group via the designated messaging channel. The format is free-text but must cover all seven required items above.

Example entry for reference (real SPC shift handover):

> 10/8 夜班交接
> 1. 86品項&未開啟：檸檬蛋糕（人員疏忽忘末下單）預計下午進貨
> 2. 促銷：起司煎餅（雞丁DF10/4）
> 3. 明日央廚會送娃娃菜10包（菜蔬沒貨）
> 4. 串燒庫存剩5分 下午來先留2份給會所預點的！
> 5. 明日預點餐已確認完畢
> 6. 蛤蜊湯不進冷凍 盤點方式更新○宣導一週
> 7. 小茹17-22請事假 / 大明今日遲到20分鐘 / 小明今日加班30分鐘協助清場

---

## Incoming Supervisor — Opening the Shift

The incoming supervisor:

- [ ] Reads the handover note before starting any prep
- [ ] Checks cold storage temperatures and logs them
- [ ] Reviews 86 items — confirms which are still closed and which can be reopened
- [ ] Checks low stock items noted in the handover
- [ ] Briefs the kitchen team on any key announcements or changes before service begins
