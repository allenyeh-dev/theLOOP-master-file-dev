## Purpose

To define how SPC handles food complaints and returned dishes — protecting the guest, the team, and the brand — while ensuring every incident is documented and investigated.

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure

**Step 1 — Retrieve the item.**
The floor team (KOR) collects the returned dish or complaint item from the guest in its entirety. The item must be returned to the kitchen complete — do not discard it.

**Step 2 — Photograph the item.**
The kitchen supervisor photographs the returned item immediately — before any handling, plating changes, or disposal. The photo is uploaded to the management group.

![Phone photographing a dish for complaint documentation](../images/05-fsh/05-fsh_013.jpg)
*Caption: All returned dishes are photographed before any action is taken. Photo is posted to the management group with the complaint report.*

**Step 3 — Ask the guest's preference.**
The floor team (KOR) checks with the guest: do they want a replacement made, or do they want the item removed from the bill (order voided)?

**Step 4 — Act on the guest's preference.**
- If the guest wants a replacement: the floor team places a new ticket; the kitchen remakes the item immediately at priority.
- If the guest wants the item removed: the floor team processes the void; no remake is required.

**Step 5 — Complete the disposal or remake paperwork.**
- If the original item is discarded: the supervisor completes a disposal form for the item.
- If a replacement is made: a new ticket is placed by the floor team before the kitchen begins.

**Step 6 — Investigate the cause.**
The supervisor investigates what caused the complaint. The report template below is completed and posted to the management group.

---

## Complaint Report Template

```
Complaint Report
Date / Time:
Complaint reason:
Prepared by (staff name):
Dispatched by (staff name):
Foreign object present: Yes / No — [describe / preserve if yes]
Root cause investigation result:
  - Staff error?
  - Supplier issue?
  - Equipment issue?
Action taken:
```

---

## Foreign Object Protocol

If a guest finds a foreign object in food:

- Do not discard the object — preserve it for investigation
- Photograph both the dish and the object
- Supervisor takes custody of the object
- Report is posted to the management group immediately
- Head Chef and Kitchen Manager are notified directly, regardless of shift

---

## Key Rules

- No returned dish is discarded before it is photographed
- All complaints — regardless of how they are resolved — are documented in the complaint report
- All complaint reports are posted to the management group before end of shift
- If the same item generates repeated complaints, the supervisor escalates to the Head Chef for a recipe or process review
