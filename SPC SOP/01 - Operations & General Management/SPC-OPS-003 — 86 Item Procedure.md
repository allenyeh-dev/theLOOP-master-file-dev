---

## Purpose

To define the procedure for closing (86ing) and reopening menu items during service, ensuring the KOR floor team and delivery platforms are informed immediately and accurately.

---

## When to 86 an Item

An item must be 86'd when:
- Ingredients are sold out and no immediate restock is available
- Ingredients are out of spec (expired, damaged, temperature breach)
- Preparation cannot be completed in time to meet service standards
- A supervisor makes the call based on quality or safety grounds

Only the on-duty supervisor may authorize an 86 during service. Kitchen staff flag the issue; the supervisor makes the call.

---

## Closing an Item (86 Procedure)

**Step 1 —** Kitchen staff identifies that an item cannot be served and notifies the supervisor immediately.

**Step 2 —** Supervisor confirms the 86 and takes the following actions simultaneously:

- [ ] Close the item on all active delivery platforms
- [ ] Call mic to notify the KOR floor team: *"[Item name] is now 86 — [brief reason if relevant]."*
- [ ] Post to the supervisor group immediately:

> **86 Report Template:**
> Item: [name]
> Reason: [e.g., 滷味86 無綜滷包可備 — braised items 86, no spice pack available]
> Time: [HH:MM]

**Step 3 —** Supervisor notes the 86 item in the end-of-shift handover note with the item name and reason.

---

## Reopening an Item

When supply is restored and the item can be served again:

**Step 1 —** Supervisor confirms the item is ready — correct ingredients available, correctly prepped, and within spec.

**Step 2 —** Reopen the item on all delivery platforms.

**Step 3 —** Call mic to notify the KOR floor team: *"[Item name] is back — now available."*

**Step 4 —** Post to the supervisor group:

> **Item Reopen Template:**
> Item: [name]
> Status: Now available
> Time: [HH:MM]

---

## LOOP Central Restock

If an 86 is caused by a shortage of an ingredient supplied by LOOP Central (央廚), the supervisor initiates a replenishment request using the template in **SPC-INV-003 — LOOP Central Replenishment Request**.

The request must include:
- Item name
- Quantity needed
- Required by date/time
- Urgency level

---

## Key Rules

- No item is served without the required ingredients — never substitute or guess
- The floor team and delivery platforms are always notified before any order can be placed for an 86'd item
- 86 items are never quietly removed — always communicate clearly
- All 86 items are logged in the shift handover note regardless of how quickly they are resolved
