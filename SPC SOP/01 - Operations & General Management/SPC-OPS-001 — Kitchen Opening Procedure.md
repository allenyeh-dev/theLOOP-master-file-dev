## Purpose

To ensure the SPC kitchen opens safely, equipment is started in the correct sequence, and all stations are ready for service before the first order is received.

---

## Opening Sequence Overview

The opening supervisor is responsible for completing all opening steps before any orders are accepted. Equipment must be started in the order listed below — do not skip or reverse steps.

| Step | Task | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ventilation & AC | Start exhaust fan, intake fan, and AC |
| 2 | Gas main valve | Open main gas supply |
| 3 | Gas cut-off alarm | Activate YAZAKI gas safety system |
| 4 | Water heater | Confirm power + open gas valve + press power |
| 5 | Dishwasher | Start per dishwasher operating instructions |
| 6 | Deep fryer | Light pilot and bring to operating temperature |
| 7 | Ovens | Power on and set correct program |

Full step-by-step procedures for each equipment item are in **SPC-EQP-001 — Equipment Opening Procedure**.

---

## Pre-Opening Checklist

Before starting equipment, the opening supervisor completes the following:

**Personal readiness:**
- [ ] Full uniform worn: white chef coat, black cotton trousers, black cap, black apron, non-slip kitchen shoes
- [ ] Hands washed per handwashing procedure (see SPC-FSH-001)
- [ ] No watches, rings, or jewellery on hands or wrists
- [ ] Nails checked — short and clean

**Venue check:**
- [ ] Kitchen entrance and all access points secured and unlocked correctly
- [ ] No water, grease, or debris on the floor from previous close
- [ ] All prep surfaces clean and dry
- [ ] Cold storage units running — check temperature gauges (cold ≤7°C, frozen ≤-18°C)

**Inventory check:**
- [ ] Review previous shift's handover note for any 86 items, pre-ordered items, or supplier follow-ups
- [ ] Check delivery schedule for the day — confirm expected LOOP Central arrival time
- [ ] Flag any low stock items to supervisor group immediately

## Shift Handover Note Review

The opening supervisor must read the previous shift's handover note before starting work. The handover note is posted in the supervisor group.

![Real SPC shift handover note — showing 86 items, items to push, staff updates, and pre-orders](../images/01-ops/01-ops_001.png)
*Caption: Example SPC shift handover note. Opening supervisor reads this before starting any prep — confirms 86 items, LOOP Central delivery status, pre-ordered items, and any staff changes.*

Key items to action from the handover note:
- **86 items** — confirm which items are closed; check if supplies are expected to arrive today
- **Items to push** — note which items are nearing expiry and should be promoted
- **Pre-ordered items** — confirm any items pre-ordered for today's service are ready or arriving
- **Supplier follow-ups** — check if any deliveries are outstanding
- **Staff notes** — be aware of any absences, late arrivals, or roster changes

---

## Opening Communication

Once the kitchen is ready for service, the supervisor:

**Step 1 —** Confirms all equipment is running and all stations are prepped.

**Step 2 —** Notifies the KOR floor team via mic: *"Kitchen is ready — now accepting orders."*

**Step 3 —** Opens delivery platform orders (see SPC-DEO-001).

**Step 4 —** Posts any 86 items or service notes to the management group before service begins.

---

## Temperature Log — Opening Entry

The opening supervisor logs cold storage temperatures at the start of every shift:

| Unit | Required Temperature | Log Filed |
|---|---|---|
| Cold storage / fridge | ≤7°C | Yes — temperature log |
| Freezer | ≤-18°C | Yes — temperature log |

If any unit is outside the required range, the supervisor notifies management immediately and does not use product from that unit until the fault is investigated.
