## Purpose

To ensure the SPC kitchen closes safely every shift — all equipment is shut down in the correct sequence, all food is stored correctly, and the kitchen is clean and ready for the next opening shift.

---

## Closing Sequence Overview

Equipment must be shut down in the order listed. Do not close the gas main valve before shutting down all gas-powered equipment.

| Step | Task |
|---|---|
| 1 | Ovens |
| 2 | Deep fryer |
| 3 | Water heater |
| 4 | Dishwasher |
| 5 | Gas main valve |
| 6 | Gas cut-off alarm |
| 7 | Ventilation, intake fan & AC |

Full step-by-step procedures for each item are in **SPC-EQP-002 — Equipment Closing Procedure**.

---

## Last Order & Service Close

**Step 1 —** At last order time, the supervisor notifies KOR floor team via mic: *"Last orders now — kitchen closing at [time]."*

**Step 2 —** All remaining delivery platform orders are confirmed closed. No new orders accepted after last order cut-off.

**Step 3 —** Any food in the danger zone (7°C–65°C) for more than 2 hours must be discarded — do not store for the next shift.

**Step 4 —** Remaining prep is cooled rapidly and stored correctly per SPC-FSH-003.

---

## Closing Checklist

**Food storage:**
- [ ] All cooked food covered and labelled with date and item name
- [ ] No unlabelled or unmarked containers in cold storage
- [ ] Fridge storage order followed — cooked food on top, raw meat on bottom
- [ ] Any items nearing expiry noted in the handover note
- [ ] Expired or out-of-spec items disposed of and disposal form completed

**Cleanliness:**
- [ ] All workbenches wiped down and dry — no standing water
- [ ] All cutting boards cleaned and sanitized through the dishwasher
- [ ] Floor mopped — drains cleared and rinsed (75% alcohol if required)
- [ ] All prep utensils cleaned and stored
- [ ] Dishwasher run, cleaned, and shut down per procedure

**Equipment:**
- [ ] All equipment shut down in closing sequence (see above)
- [ ] Gas main valve confirmed closed
- [ ] Gas cut-off alarm confirmed off
- [ ] Ventilation and AC confirmed off

**Inventory:**
- [ ] Stock levels checked — any critically low items noted for handover
- [ ] Delivery schedule reviewed for the next day — any pre-orders placed or confirmed

---

## Shift Handover Note

The closing supervisor writes the shift handover note before leaving. The note is posted to the supervisor group and covers:

- **86 items & unopened items** — what is closed and why (e.g., sold out, waiting for delivery)
- **Items to push** — any items with a short remaining shelf life that the next shift should promote
- **Supplier issues / outstanding deliveries** — any items that didn't arrive or need follow-up
- **Low stock alerts** — any items the next shift should watch
- **Pre-ordered items for the next day** — what is confirmed or needs confirmation
- **Key announcements** — any procedures or policy updates to communicate
- **Staff notes** — any absences, lateness, overtime, or HR items

The handover note must be posted before the closing supervisor leaves the kitchen.
