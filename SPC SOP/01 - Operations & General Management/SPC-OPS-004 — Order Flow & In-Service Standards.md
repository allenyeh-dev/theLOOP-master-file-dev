## Purpose

To define how orders are received, processed, and dispatched at SPC, and to establish the non-negotiable rules that govern in-service conduct.

---

## Order Sources

SPC receives orders from two sources:

| Source | System | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| KOR dine-in guests | KOR POS → kitchen ticket | Orders placed by KOR floor team on behalf of guests |
| Delivery customers | Delivery platform tablets | Orders received directly via delivery platforms |

All orders must be ticketed before food preparation begins. No food is prepared or dispatched without a confirmed ticket.

---

## Order Processing Rules

**Rule 1 — No ticket, no food.**
Food preparation begins only after a valid order ticket is received. Pre-making items and filling in the ticket afterwards is prohibited. Any violation — if confirmed — requires the supervisor and relevant floor staff to purchase the item at full price.

**Rule 2 — No order, no dispatch.**
Food is not dispatched to the floor or delivery rider without a confirmed order ticket. Sending food without a ticket and filling it in later is not permitted under any circumstances.

**Rule 3 — Delays are communicated proactively.**
If order volume is high and dispatch will be delayed, the supervisor notifies the KOR floor team before guests ask. The supervisor communicates the expected wait time and number of affected items. Do not wait to be asked — communicate first.

**Rule 4 — All tickets are fulfilled in order.**
Tickets are processed in the sequence they are received. Skipping or reordering tickets without supervisor approval is not allowed.

---

## During Service — Supervisor Responsibilities

The on-duty supervisor monitors the following throughout service:

- [ ] Order tickets are being processed in the correct sequence
- [ ] No prep bottlenecks developing at any station
- [ ] Ingredient levels are adequate — flag low stock before it becomes an 86
- [ ] All dispatched items meet quality and presentation standards
- [ ] Any delays are communicated to the KOR floor team proactively
- [ ] Temperature logs are completed at required intervals

---

## Dispatch Standards

Before any item leaves the kitchen:

- Correct item matches the ticket
- Portion size and presentation meet the standard for that item
- Temperature is correct — hot food is hot, cold food is cold
- Packaging is complete and sealed for delivery orders
- Labelling is correct on delivery orders

---

## Issue Escalation During Service

If any issue cannot be resolved at the kitchen level during service:

**Step 1 —** Supervisor handles the immediate issue (e.g., holds the order, redirects prep).

**Step 2 —** Supervisor notifies the relevant parties (KOR floor team, management group) depending on the nature of the issue.

**Step 3 —** Supervisor documents the issue in the shift handover note.

Refer to **SPC-OPS-005** for guest complaint handling, and **SPC-OPS-003** for 86 procedures.
